We doubled Friday orders at our Lalitpur bakery with one change.

We stopped posting cake photos and started posting the 6am prep. Flour on the table, Sita icing 40 cupcakes, the oven timer.

People do not want a perfect cake. They want to see who made it.

Three weeks in, Friday orders went from 22 to 47. Same menu. Same prices.

What would you want to see from a bakery at 6am?
