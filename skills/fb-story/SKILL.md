# fb-story

Stories are for the people who already follow you. They're 24-hour, low
production, and the best way to start private conversations, because a reply
to a story lands in Messenger.

## The daily shape (3-7 frames)

```
1  OPEN       what's happening today, in one line and one real image/video
2-4 BODY      behind the scenes, process, a customer moment, a tip
5  INTERACT   one sticker: poll, question, or a direct "reply with X"
6  ASK        the one action: message us, see the post, book
```

Rules of thumb:

- **One frame, one idea**, readable in ~2 seconds. 6-10 words of text max.
- **Real over polished.** Phone video and natural light outperform designed
  graphics here.
- **Text inside the safe zone**: away from the top ~250px and bottom ~340px of
  1080x1920, where the name and reply bar sit.
- If the account cross-posts Instagram stories to Facebook, check stickers:
  not every sticker carries over. Plan the Facebook version explicitly.

## Which sticker does which job

| sticker / tool | job |
|---|---|
| Poll | low-effort first interaction; teaches people to tap |
| Question | collects objections and FAQ for future posts |
| Link | sends traffic; use sparingly, one per day |
| "Reply with WORD" (text) | starts a Messenger conversation, the real goal |
| Music / location | context; location helps local discovery |

## The Messenger funnel

A story reply opens a conversation the user can answer. Plan the reply:

1. Story frame: "Reply MENU and I'll send this week's prices."
2. The reply they send back, written now in the user's voice (short, human,
   no link-dump in the first message). Use `/fb-dm`.
3. A follow-up question that moves toward the action.

Automated replies are only allowed through Meta's own tools (Business Suite
automations) or approved partners, and only after the person messages first.

## Output

A frame-by-frame table: frame, visual, on-screen text, sticker, and the
prepared Messenger reply. Run text through `/fb-human`. Nothing is posted.
